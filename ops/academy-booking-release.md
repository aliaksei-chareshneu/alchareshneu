## Formula and timestamps
- Use **server current time** and `starts_at` as timezone-aware instants. For provisional amount: `ceil(base * 0.25)` if `now() > starts_at - interval '48 hours'`, else zero. Exactly 48 hours is NOT late.
- Final surcharge depends on the **bank verified credited timestamp**, not PDF file mtime, email, user-selected date, or client clock. A late uploader who paid early must not pay the surcharge. An early uploader whose transfer arrives late may owe it (subject to previously agreed terms).
- If no slots or API/storage access, stop in state BLOCKED / NO BOOKING and do not switch to an unverified alternative form automatically.
