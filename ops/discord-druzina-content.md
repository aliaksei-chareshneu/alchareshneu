## welcome (owner-approved posting only)

Welcome to **Druzina Moravy**! We are a Brno-based community for people curious about historical fencing (HEMA), fantasy, LARP, teamwork, and the making of historical-style equipment.

New here? Read **#rules-and-safety**, introduce yourself only if you wish, and explore **#first-training**. Our current public programme and participation information are on the official page:
https://aliaksei-chareshneu.github.io/alchareshneu/druzina/

Joining this Discord server is optional. It does **not** make you a statutory member of Druzina Moravy z.s., confirm a place at training, or prove any payment or qualification. Each activity has its own organiser confirmation, location, conditions and safety requirements. If you are below Discord's minimum age for your country, please use an alternative organiser contact route.

## rules-and-safety (owner-approved posting only)

1. Treat other people respectfully. No harassment, bullying, threats, hateful content or unsolicited DMs.
2. Protect privacy. Do not post members' phone numbers, financial details, addresses, school information or identifiable photos without permission. No photos of minors without the lawful controller's approval and appropriate permission.
3. Safety first: only attend supervised activities with organiser confirmation; respect equipment and site instructions. Messages or emoji reactions are not registrations, insurance or training approval.
4. No commercial spam, unapproved partner advertising, illegal transactions or dangerous practical instructions.
5. Ask moderators before sharing other organisations' member lists, events or invites. Moderators may remove unsafe content and restrict access in line with the published rules.
