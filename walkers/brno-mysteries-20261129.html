One city. Seven stories.

  1. Měnínská brána — surviving medieval gateway and meeting point
  2. Kapucínské náměstí — mysteries of the Capuchins, outside only
  3. Petrov Cathedral — sunset views and the famous 11 o’clock legend
  4. Old Town Hall — Brno’s dragon and the crooked turret
  5. Dominikánské náměstí — the festive city
  6. Náměstí Svobody — Christmas lights and unusual local stories
  7. Zelný trh — finish among the Christmas stalls, near the 17:00 public music programme

Meet here

15:30 outside Měnínská brána, Měnínská 7, Brno. Stay on the public pavement without blocking the entrance. Detailed joining instructions follow personal confirmation.

Included: 90 minutes of live English-language guiding and small interactive mysteries. Not included: food, drinks, transport or optional paid entrances (none required). All stops are outdoors.
