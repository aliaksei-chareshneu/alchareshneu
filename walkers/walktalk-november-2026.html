Request a place · 18 Nov

How this works

  1. Apply on this website for either date. Your request is not yet a booking.
  2. We confirm individually only when we have at least 6 real participants, a co-host and a safe weather/route check. Decisions by 13 Nov 18:00 for Sunday and 16 Nov 18:00 for Wednesday (CET).
  3. Pay 50 Kč to the organiser on arrival, only after receiving personal written event and place confirmation. No advance payments; no travel until confirmed.

Changing your mind before the walk? Tell us; there is no cancellation or 120 Kč processing fee for these two 50 Kč micro-walks. If we cancel for unsafe weather or too few people, nothing is charged. English-first with Czech support (other languages by arrangement). We do not exchange people's personal contacts or photograph them without consent. A community conversation walk, not a language lesson or clinical service.
