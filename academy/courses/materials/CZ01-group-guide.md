# CZ01 — Czech A1: Start Speaking
## Online small-group course · 10 × 60 minutes

**Status:** ready as a ten-lesson teaching plan and first-lesson worksheet; group is not scheduled or sold until a compatible level, 3–4 people, ten dates, price, payment and cancellation terms are confirmed.
**Existing published Academy small-group rate:** 3,150 Kč per participant for 10 online lessons of 60 minutes; start requires at least 3 mutually compatible paid places.
**Languages of explanation:** Czech, English or Russian, matched to the group.
**Outcome:** produce and understand basic self-introductions, questions, common numbers and everyday transactions. This does not guarantee CEFR level certification.

### Programme
1. Sounds, polite greetings, `Jmenuji se...`, `Odkud jste?`
2. Personal information, numbers and spelling.
3. `Jsem / jste`, people and countries.
4. Family and basic descriptive adjectives.
5. Times, dates and appointments.
6. Shopping, price and quantity.
7. Food and ordering politely.
8. Directions and public transport.
9. Simple everyday messages and dialogues.
10. Practical speaking assessment, feedback and next-step plan.

### Lesson 1 (60 minutes)
- 0–7: warm welcome, learner baseline (each speaks 20 seconds).
- 7–16: basic Czech sounds; practise `č`, `š`, `ž`, long vowels.
- 16–26: model:
  - A: `Dobrý den. Jak se jmenujete?`
  - B: `Jmenuji se Anna. A vy?`
  - A: `Já jsem Petr. Těší mě.`
  - B: `Mě také.`
- 26–39: guided substitutions with participants' chosen fictional names; switch partners.
- 39–49: role-play meeting a new neighbour. Add `Odkud jste?` — `Jsem z Brna / z Prahy / z Ukrajiny.`
- 49–56: mini-check without notes (4 questions).
- 56–60: feedback and exact homework.

### Student exercises
1. Complete: `Dobrý den. Jak se ___?` — `Jmenuji se Eva.`
2. Choose the polite answer to `Odkud jste?`: (a) `Děkuji`, (b) `Jsem z Brna`, (c) `Nashledanou`.
3. Write a two-person introduction with a greeting, name, country/city and farewell.
4. Practise aloud: `Dobrý den`, `Těší mě`, `Na shledanou`.

**Answer key:** (1) `jmenujete`; (2) b; (3) assess appropriateness, understandable word order and polite forms; (4) instructor checks pronunciation and meaningful corrections.

**Homework, 10–15 minutes:** record or rehearse a 40-second self-introduction with your name, city, preferred language and one greeting. Do not submit voice recordings publicly; share only using an agreed private channel with appropriate consent.

### Teacher notes
Use quick correction rather than interrupting every utterance. Each learner should speak for at least 8 minutes in lesson 1. Record a baseline and compare with the final role-play; do not rank learners publicly or reuse voices/photos without permission.
