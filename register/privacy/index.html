Controller and contact

Your event's data controller is identified in the registration form before submission. Družina Moravy, z. s. and commercial services provided by Aliaksei Chareshneu as OSVČ are separate organizers and legal entities. Contact for questions or data rights: chereshnev.biochem@gmail.com.

Data and purpose

The event registration form asks for name, surname, email, telephone, age category, participation intention and confirmation of safety rules. Minors need a separate guardian contact and organizer verification. The organizer uses these details to handle your requested event registration, safety logistics, changes and cancellations. The legal basis is the arrangement of requested participation and, where applicable, legitimate operational duties.

Optional permissions

Publishing identifiable photos or video on the organizer's website and social media requires a separate optional YES choice, and saying NO will not exclude anyone. Future event invitations require another separately optional opt-in. You may withdraw either permission by contacting the organizer. A child's photo permission requires guardian approval separately.

Storage and retention

Registration records are handled by authorized organizers in the existing access-controlled Google Sheets CRM, using Google Apps Script as a backend. They are not published on the website. Ordinary registration details are scheduled for deletion or anonymization no later than 90 days after the event. Where the participant also requests future invitations, the organizer may retain basic contact and relevant event-interest details for up to 12 months from the latest voluntary interaction, or until consent is withdrawn. Data needed for specific legal obligations may require different retention. The cleanup job must be installed and checked by the organizer.

Rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or objection as applicable, and withdraw optional consent. Complaints may be directed to the Czech Data Protection Authority, ÚOOÚ. Registration does not establish paid booking, insurance, legal membership or admission to supervised sports.
