Event registration · Privacy

Updated 25 October 2026. The displayed organizer for both currently listed free events is Družina Moravy, z. s. (community project Morava v pohybu / BrnoWalkers). This is separate from Aliaksei Chareshneu's commercial OSVČ services.

Information collected

First name, surname, email, phone, selected event, attendance intention, safety acknowledgement, age group and two independent optional permissions (photo publication and future event invitations). A guardian name and phone are requested for minors.

Where it goes

This static webpage does not submit, store or transmit your entries automatically. When you choose WhatsApp or email, it prepares a message; data go to the organizer only if you press Send in that app. WhatsApp or your email provider processes messages under its own privacy terms. The organizer may then enter received registration details manually into an access-controlled Google Sheets CRM for the event.

Purpose and choices

Essential details are needed to respond to the requested event registration, confirm safety conditions and announce necessary changes. Photo publication is optional and independent, and saying no does not affect participation. Future event invitations are another optional choice, limited to the named organizer, with withdrawal on request. They do not authorize sharing contact lists for unrelated commercial advertising. For minors, the organizer separately verifies guardian permission; minors cannot independently authorize public photos or marketing.

Retention

Event registration details are normally anonymized within 90 days after the event unless a distinct legal retention duty applies. If you opted into future invitations, minimal contact information and consent evidence can be retained for up to 12 months after your latest voluntary interaction, or until you withdraw. Retention requires organizer-controlled cleanup; contact us to request an earlier deletion.

Contact and rights

For access, correction, removal, objection, or withdrawal of optional consent, email chereshnev.biochem@gmail.com. You may also contact the Czech Data Protection Authority (ÚOOÚ).
