Bez souhlasu se údaje nepoužívají k reklamním rozesílkám.

Pro opravu, zrušení účasti, odvolání dobrovolného souhlasu, výmaz, přístup či námitku napište na uvedený e-mail. Z důvodu ochrany před zneužitím opakované anonymní odeslání automaticky nepřepisuje původní žádost ani souhlasy. Dozor: ÚOOÚ.

English

Controller: the particular organizer shown next to the selected event, e.g. Družina Moravy, z. s. The association and Aliaksei Chareshneu's separate OSVČ services are different legal operators. Questions and data rights: chereshnev.biochem@gmail.com.

We collect first/surname, email, phone, event choice, age group, intended attendance and safety-rule acknowledgment to process a requested RSVP, coordinate changes and organize the event safely. A minor requires a guardian contact. An RSVP is not a confirmed booking, insurance, paid transaction, or association membership.

Photo/video publication consent is an optional separate choice. Occasional event invitations are another separate opt-in request; no mailing starts until verified. Minors' publicity permission requires separate guardian authorization; a child's submission cannot grant it. Saying NO does not exclude participation.

Event contacts are stored in an access-controlled Supabase database located in the EU (Frankfurt). Ordinary RSVP records are deleted 90 days after an event. If future invitations are requested, only name, email and organizer are held in a separate pending-confirmation table for up to 365 days or until withdrawal. Access is restricted to authorized organizers. No unconditional mailing-list enrollment or sale of personal data occurs.

To correct/cancel a request or withdraw optional consent, contact the organizer. Anonymous duplicate submissions never overwrite previous contacts or permissions. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure or object, and complain to the Czech authority ÚOOÚ.
