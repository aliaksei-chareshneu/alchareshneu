Event registration · Privacy

Updated 27 October 2026. The displayed organizer for both currently listed free events is Družina Moravy, z. s. (community project Morava v pohybu / BrnoWalkers). This is separate from Aliaksei Chareshneu's commercial OSVČ services.

Information collected

First name, surname, email, phone, selected event, attendance intention, safety acknowledgement, age group and two independent optional permissions (photo publication and future event invitations). A guardian name and phone are requested for minors.

Where it goes

When you select an event open for online registration and press Send RSVP securely, the page sends the entered registration data over HTTPS to the privately managed IMPERION RSVP endpoint on Supabase (EU project). The endpoint validates the event and stores the request in a protected database; anonymous visitors have no permission to read registration records. The organiser may then transfer records into an access-controlled Google Sheets CRM. This online request is not an automatic reservation, attendance confirmation or payment request. Other events labelled for direct contact, and the optional email contact button, open WhatsApp or the email app instead; a message is sent only after you press Send in that separate app and is not automatically stored in the RSVP database. Avoid submitting a request repeatedly if a network error occurs.

Purpose and choices

Essential details are needed to respond to the requested event registration, confirm safety conditions and announce necessary changes. Photo publication is optional and independent, and saying no does not affect participation. Future event invitations are another optional choice, limited to the named organizer, with withdrawal on request. They do not authorize sharing contact lists for unrelated commercial advertising. For minors, the organizer separately verifies guardian permission; minors cannot independently authorize public photos or marketing.

Retention

Online RSVPs are assigned an intended retention deadline of 90 days after the event, with separate obligations if the law requires them. The organiser is responsible for enforcing deletion or anonymisation in the database and any CRM copies; merely storing an expiry date does not itself delete a record. Optional future invitations require a separate subsequent confirmation before entering an active mailing list; pending opt-in requests normally expire within 12 months or sooner upon withdrawal. Contact us to request deletion, correction or details of the active retention arrangements.

Contact and rights

For access, correction, removal, objection, or withdrawal of optional consent, email chereshnev.biochem@gmail.com. You may also contact the Czech Data Protection Authority (ÚOOÚ).
