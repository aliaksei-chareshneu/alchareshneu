Safety rules for community hikes

Version 25 October 2026. These basic conditions apply to outdoor walking events; each event may have additional instructions. Specific historical fencing, HEMA, archery and LARP events have their own activity-specific safety rules.

  • Confirm the date, route, meeting point, weather and required equipment with the organizer; attendance needs explicit organizer confirmation.
  • Wear appropriate shoes and clothing and bring water, weather protection and any essential items.
  • Follow the leader's safety instructions, stay on safe paths and avoid restricted or dangerous areas. Inform the leader before leaving the group.
  • Do not participate while intoxicated or otherwise unable to manage the planned activity safely.
  • Notify the leader promptly of an injury, problem, lost participant or unsafe situation, and stop when instructed.
  • For minors, suitable guardian permission and any additional safeguards must be verified by the organizer before participation.

Reading and accepting these rules is not a waiver of statutory rights, proof of insurance or automatic admission. Contact the organizer for individual arrangements.
