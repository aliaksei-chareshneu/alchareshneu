Safety rules for community events

Version 2026-10-25-v1. These are the basic common rules for outdoor walks, cultural events and supervised group experiences in Brno. A specific event may have further safety and age restrictions.

Before arriving

  • Confirm the correct date, meeting point, route/difficulty, equipment, fee and any weather changes with the organizer.
  • Wear suitable clothing and shoes, bring sufficient water and any personal essentials appropriate for the event.
  • Do not attend under the influence of alcohol or drugs. If you are unwell or cannot safely manage the advertised activity, contact the organizer privately before participating.
  • For anyone under 18, the organizer must verify guardian permission and any activity-specific safeguards before participation.

During the event

  • Follow instructions from the organizer and designated supervisors; never enter restricted or unsafe areas.
  • Inform the organizer immediately of injuries, unsafe conditions, lost participants or equipment problems. Stop when instructed.
  • Respect the group's pace, other participants, the environment and any local venue rules. Do not leave the group without notifying the leader where required for safety.
  • Some activities involve natural or physical risks; registration does not eliminate these risks or establish insurance coverage.

Additional rules for fencing, archery and LARP

General acknowledgement on this page is NOT sufficient permission for contact training, archery, a paid sports session or minors. The organizer must confirm the exact event, qualified supervision, required protective equipment, insurance status and any separate briefing or guardian agreement. See the Družina Moravy sport-specific safety information and club rules.

Important: A safety acknowledgement confirms the participant has read and will follow these rules. It is not a blanket waiver of legal rights or of an organizer's responsibility. Registration records interest and intended attendance; the organizer confirms actual admission separately.

Questions or concerns? Contact the organizer.
