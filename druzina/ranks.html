Ranks & Achievements

Three qualifications. One fellowship.

Ranks are earned; temporary command roles are assigned separately. Junior / Senior is a current participation tariff, not a combat rank.

Code / TitleQualification / requirementBand
B-I RecruitFirst training + safety briefingNone

Senior = green thin detachable ribbon (tariff, not rank). B-I none; B-II red; B-III blue; B-IV orange; B-V black/gold.
