# Družina Moravy — evidence-based 90-day member experience (pilot, NOT automatic messaging)

**Version:** 2026-10-21 / research-informed design for 2026-11-01 through 2027-01-17.

**Status:** INTERNAL OPERATIONAL SOP PROPOSAL. Existing approved rank/attendance/price/consent policies are not replaced. **No new paid HEMA sales, instructor authorizations, consent collection, messages or membership qualifications are activated by this document.**

## Objective and evidence threshold

The club's first operational objective is a **safe, voluntary, enjoyable return** (2nd verified real attended session within 30 days), followed by useful, self-directed involvement and 90-day engagement. Attendance is not synonymous with statutory membership, payment, rank eligibility or contact consent. All metrics use **matured cohorts** and verifiable session dates only.

This is informed by sport dropout/autonomy/competence literature and official sport-volunteering guidance. Associations and youth studies provide directional evidence, not a demonstrated causal effect in adult Czech HEMA/LARP; we must validate locally.

## The member experience — five supported moments

| Moment | Goal | Trainer or helper behavior | Data evidence | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First enquiry | Lower uncertainty; clarify safety and access | Answer real question, likely format, indicative days, safe dress; ask preferred information channel only when necessary | Valid enquiry timestamp/source if lawfully recorded | Enquiry not booking; no payment link or claims of confirmed session |
| First **verified** attendance | Help the person feel welcome, safe and capable | Introduce consenting buddy, demonstrate STOP, distance and simple controlled success, offer genuinely optional sparring | A real trainer-sign-off of actual session ID and safety induction | Instructor, venue, insurance, age/guardian and activity safety verified |
| Days 1–3 after first attendance | Reduce confusion about what comes next | One personalised practical message **only if lawful basis / permitted channel**; confirm next date if actually confirmed; allow “not now” | Last communication, basis and opt-out recorded; never infer contact permission from follower status | No unsolicited promotion, no promise of booking or tariff |
| Second / fourth attended session | Build autonomy and competence | Visit 2: optional track exploration (line / separately supervised archery). Visit 4: formative feedback, one achievable next skill; not a paid compulsory 4-pack or final exam | Two distinct trainer-verified sessions, observed skill, one self-chosen goal | Track specialty and equipment authorization; earned B/C/K separate |
| Days 30/60/90 | Sustainable belonging instead of pressure | Offer optional micro-role (repair, greeting, logistics, media only with consent, history/board games), pause or change format; do not chase non-responders repeatedly | Opt-in, participant-proposed interests, last visit date, accepted roles only | Avoid profiling private health, age etc.; do not auto assign "Senior" volunteer obligations |

## Practical 90-minute training design

- 0–15 min: welcome, STOP, body/weapon/venue checks, adapted option for newcomer.
- 15–30 min: one **clear achievable skill** for everybody, novice/experienced variants.
- 30–65 min: cyclic technique / groupfight / scenario (Mon–Thu–Sat rotating across 3 weeks). Individual opt-out, matched partners, instructor's safety limits.
- 65–80 min: low-risk team application; separate archery range only with approved dedicated supervision.
- 80–90 min: honest debrief, perceived competence (“what improved?”), member-chosen next action.
- Each week exactly one Pěšák theme, not 3 new credits for attending formats; >=9 distinct verified themes + practical check; no automatic awards.
- Change or cancel tentative dates if staff/venue/insurance not confirmed. No fake attendance from planned calendar sessions.

## Retention metrics: denominator discipline

1. **First-to-second within 30d** = unique people with a **verified first visit date at least 30 days before cutoff** and a second distinct verified visit within 30d / unique first visitors matured 30d. Deduplicate Person_ID; exclude duplicate session rows.
2. **Fourth within 90d** = unique people with >=4 genuine distinct visits within 90d / first visit cohort matured 90d.
3. **90-day return** = unique people with one actual session in days **61–90** after first visit / first visitors matured 90d; not "active since 90 days".
4. **Repeat active weeks** = unique weeks with real verified attendance, not calendar invitations.
5. **B/C/K** = separate independently reviewed competences, never based on "rank XP", purchases or social popularity.
6. **Cancellations & care** = track whether sessions actually occurred; classify club cancellations separately from personal no-shows.
7. **Verified recurring cash** = separate legal sellers, cash received/refunds/holds and month; do not report a proposed Stripe/plan as MRR.
8. **Volunteer experience** = opted-in roles, hours if independently evidenced, gratitude and an easy no/exit; no minimum coerced volunteering. Existing **Senior tariff** rights/obligations are separate and cannot silently change.

With empty attendance ledgers, first-to-second / 90-day metrics remain **N/A**, not 0%.

## Segmentation without stigmatizing members

- Newcomer (one verified session): caring human welcome / optional beginner track.
- Repeat regular (2+): one tangible skill and one genuine social connection.
- Event-only or infrequent: keep an occasional safe route; don't call inactive a failure.
- Volunteer or maker: opt-in micro-roles, skills growth, proper recognition and offboarding.
- Paused person: no automatic nag sequence; at most one lawful, relevant nonpressuring contact depending on channel, purpose and preferences.
- Minors: guardian, age-appropriate lead and safeguarding checks. Never disclose attendance publicly by default.

## Contact and privacy — Czech jurisdiction

The Czech ÚOOÚ guidance makes an **important distinction**: necessary association administration and ordinary lawful member communications are not necessarily based on explicit GDPR consent, whereas publishing a list of members, using photos for unrelated purposes, or marketing disclosures may require separate lawful grounds/permissions. There is **no universal "consent for every service reminder" rule**. Record the **actual lawful basis by purpose**, use data minimization/notice, respect objection and opt-out. Never use scraping or Telegram member exports as permission to mass-market.

Operational notices about a confirmed, previously requested session differ from a promotional acquisition campaign. For marketing-like automated contact, obtain an appropriate legal review and applicable opt-in. For media, follow individual age/guardian/portrait-permission rules.

## Pilot / safe deployment checks

- **Oct 2026:** verify member IDs, actual previous attendance, current safety form/rules, trainer and venue. Do not blanket import email/phones from disparate chats.
- **Nov 1:** voluntary member-facing pilot for up to 5–10 consenting existing members with qualified human review.
- **Nov 2 onward:** rotating training content only on actually verified sessions.
- **Nov 19:** review matured first/second attendance only where valid baseline exists, welcome experience, member feedback, safety incidents and unresolved questions; no fake uplift.
- **Dec 19:** review opt-in micro-roles, member friction and actual cost/runway.
- **Jan 17 2027:** review cohort retention, adverse events, coach workload and measured renewal revenue. End or revise pilot if fails. Do not call small cohort changes statistically established improvements.

**Stop conditions:** no verified trainer/venue/insurance, unapproved activity, missing guardian consent where required, unresolved price/contract ambiguity, signs of harassment/coercion, unsafe exposure of contact/attendance data. No auto-send, recurring billing or new paid HEMA booking until all relevant existing P0 gates are satisfied.

## Small weekly trainer checklist

1. Who actually attended? Record unique verified session ID and date (trainer checked).
2. Were STOP, equipment, consent/guardian and safety issues checked?
3. Did newcomers receive an individually achievable task and a **choice** about their preferred route?
4. Was a social connection/buddy **voluntary** and appropriate?
5. Did anyone ask to slow down, avoid sparring, pause, return to a different format or take a craft role?
6. Which next dates are **confirmed**, not merely planned?
7. Is there a private, nonidentifying incident/feedback route and a responsible human?
8. No automatic promotions, photos, marketing blasts or debt collection from informal records.
