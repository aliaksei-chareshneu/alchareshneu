## Ten hard conclusions (ranking: retention > acquisition > monetization)

1. **An achievable welcome matters more than decorative gamification.** Youth sport longitudinal/meta-analytic work identifies enjoyment, persistence intention, supportive coaching and perceived ability as relevant. Adult group exercise evidence cautions that relationships alone do not guarantee adherence. Implement one achievable first-session objective, member-paced challenge and a choice after the basics. Do not require competition or sparring.  
   Peer and government sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9196305/ · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11684458/ · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13295748/ · https://www.ausport.gov.au/clearinghouse/evidence/children-and-youth-in-sport/factors/coaching
2. **Member success is a REAL second visit, not a newsletter sign-up.** Record only independently verified attended sessions, separate first, second and fourth milestone. 30/90-day retention denominator includes only mature cohorts. With no trusted logs, KPIs are **N/A**, not 0%.  
   Source: https://www.ausport.gov.au/clearinghouse/evidence/major-events/engagement/sport-participation
3. **Time, travel, equipment and compulsory volunteer labour are real costs.** An attractive discount can still lose money/people if it extracts mandatory support work. Review Senior tariff effort honestly; do not silently change contract rights, refunds or existing rank progress. Offer occasional member roles with choice.  
   Sources: https://www.ausport.gov.au/clearinghouse/evidence/factors-influencing-sport-participation/cost · https://www.ausport.gov.au/clearinghouse/evidence/children-and-youth-in-sport/factors/barriers
4. **Volunteer jobs must have one safe, limited, trainable scope.** An organiser needs a role description, briefing, support, acknowledgement and easy exit. A temporary duty is NOT a permanent C command qualification, nor a forced subscription benefit.  
   Sources: https://www.ausport.gov.au/clearinghouse/evidence/volunteers-in-sport/management · https://www.volunteeringaustralia.org/get-involved/resources/nationalstandards/
5. **Acquisition is capacity-constrained.** Before advertising a battle to thousands, ensure every club can genuinely support welcoming newcomers safely: named qualified trainer, legal venue, insurance, safe stock, supervision, accessible paths and a credible confirmed next date. Hundreds of interested spectators are not members or bookable slots.  
   Source: https://www.ausport.gov.au/clearinghouse/evidence/major-events/engagement/sport-participation
6. **Privacy needs purpose-specific bases, not consent checkbox maximalism.** Czech ÚOOÚ guidance distinguishes necessary club administration from optional marketing, images and publication of member lists. Never bulk import Telegram contacts or conflate public profile with training consent.  
   Sources: https://uoou.gov.cz/profesional/qa-otazky-a-odpovedi/cinnosti-spolku-sportovnich-klubu-a-obdobnych-zajmovych-sdruzeni · https://uoou.gov.cz/verejnost/qa-otazky-a-odpovedi/k-vyzadovani-souhlasu
7. **A large battle is not a big local practice.** Dedicated safety supervision, venue access, emergency plans, crowd/traffic separation, archery safety and age-appropriate protections must precede scale; different combat modalities are not simply combined. Reference international practice but obtain Czech-specific legal/insurance clearance.  
   Sources: https://www.safesportinternational.com/standards/international-safeguards-for-adults-in-sport/ · https://www.coe.int/en/web/sport/child-safeguarding · https://www.hse.gov.uk/event-safety/crowd-management-assess.htm
8. **An alliance is more scalable than founder-operated chapters.** Approve an actual cell only after local team, instructor, venue, safety, finance and brand responsibilities are proven. Approach independent clubs as equal partners, not unilaterally as subsidiaries.  
   Sources: https://www.ffamhe.fr/ · https://sportnz.org.nz/resources/governance-lite/
9. **A full-time "service" needs cash, not vanity counters.** Funds must be attributed to the correct legal recipient (z.s. vs OSVČ). Recurring institutional grants/contracts differ from project grants and cannot be counted before approval. Any paid staff role requires funded runway and financially reviewed job scope.  
   Sources: https://www.sportengland.org/funding-and-campaigns/code-sports-governance · https://cep.org/blog/assessing-impact-with-general-operating-support-grants-debunking-the-myths/
10. **Website success is task completion, not a redesigned hero.** Four-language newbie journey + clear "enquiry, not booking" + volunteer/chapter/sponsor routes already exist; user tests remain required. Respect WCAG reflow, touch targets, focus, reduced motion, accessible language and plain outcomes. No Event rich data for dates lacking legally verified organizer, venue and trainer.  
   Sources: https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-4-make-the-service-simple-to-use · https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow · https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/event
