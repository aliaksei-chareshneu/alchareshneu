# Družina Moravy — regional chapter and event readiness gates

**2026-10-21 · internal controlled proposal; not a franchise offer, charter, legal opinion, or evidence that any additional chapter/event has been authorized.**

## Strategic objective

A **federated alliance** of safe groups and shared resources is more plausible than 100,000 independent Czech HEMA trainees in one organization. Cooperate with existing clubs; do not present groups as affiliates without their explicit written agreement. Separate (A) **audience/followers**, (B) **participating members**, (C) **monthly training attendees**, (D) **paying members**, (E) **regional independent partners**, (F) **verified affiliated chapters** and (G) **event audience and actual combatants**. No combining these in one growth figure.

## Chapter acceptance checklist — all requirements mandatory before public "active chapter"

| Gate | Evidence | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| C0. Local lead | Written identity, authority, capacity for reliable operations | Anonymous aspirant or founder-dependent volunteer only |
| C1. Governance | Charter for responsibility / reporting / conflicts of interest / use of brand; approved by legitimate organ | No signed terms, unclear legal entity or accountability |
| C2. Trained safe instructor | Named qualified trainer(s) for actual weapons/formats and emergency STOP, substitute coverage | Inexperienced volunteer called "coach" without checks |
| C3. Venue | Written permission, safe capacity, surfaces, exits, protective zones and storage | No written authorization or unsafe building |
| C4. Insurance/organizer | Activity-, locale-, age- and event-specific insurance, legal seller if any paid offering, clear payment beneficiary | Insurance not verified; paying HEMA using unauthorized seller |
| C5. Safeguarding | Adolescent/child guardian consent, safe recruitment, reporting mechanism, no unsupervised sensitive situations, adapted adult protection | No designated qualified safeguarding contact or unsafe practice |
| C6. Finance | Separate cash accounts, monthly ledger, approval thresholds, conflicts, receipt reconciliation, refund rights | Founder or one person controls cash without review |
| C7. Operational team | Minimum three distinct locally consenting operations contributors for a pilot as a governance **target** | Single overextended founder promises a chapter |
| C8. Real pilot | A small, approved low-risk practice/demo with documented incident handling and member feedback | Only virtual members, event guesses or fabricated attendance |
| C9. Independent review | Founder's/board's explicit approval and a 30/90 day review; right to suspend brand/activities | No audit trail or review/exit process |

## Crowd/event scale ("thousands of participants" is a separate organization)

- Each proposed scale-up needs professional venue, traffic, public-authority, sanitary and emergency planning relevant to Czech law; these requirements **cannot be inferred from UK HSE guidance**. Use HSE as a checklist for physical hazards only.
- Plan separately for on-field fighters, screened spectators, volunteers, marshals, first-aid teams, children, vehicles, overnight accommodation, noise, fire, archery range perimeter and environmental impact.
- Validate independent medical and event-control capacity; published target attendance is not evidence of attendance.
- Tier upgrades must be **earned through completed and safely audited events**, not a marketing target. Suggested internal milestones 30→100→300→1,000→3,000+ total people are *planning gates* only, not safety standards.
- For mixed events, apply the **most restrictive specific weapon/safety rules** and separate incompatible formats (HEMA steel vs foam LARP vs archery vs buhurt); no improvised combined fighting field.

## Governance and money — recurring support without financial dependence

1. Map each source to the legal entity **z.s. vs OSVČ**, activity, tax treatment, grant restrictions, reporting duty, approved bank and decision authority. Do not mingle grants with personal income.
2. Base one paid staff position on actual repeated receipts, a costed role, written appointment rights, direct supervision, and **at least 3–6 months funded runway** (scenario target, not a statutory rule).
3. Diversify: proven lawful member dues/participation, recurring annual framework contracts for permissible services, ongoing unrestricted sponsorship/support, and public project grants as supplements. Do not describe grants as contracts.
4. Distinguish volunteer from employee and from remunerated contractor; no unapproved unpaid labor "required" by a tariff. Preserve previously agreed rights until explicit valid change.
5. Written conflicts policy: committee/founder dual roles and related-party invoices need independent decision, market reasonableness, disclosures, accounting and local counsel if needed.
6. Approved public event announcements **only after** venue, trainer, format, safety, insurer and legal seller clarity. No online paid HEMA checkout before existing business P0 legal gate.
7. Each **chapter** has a local budget and responsible team; cannot use aggregated unverified revenue to justify a central wage.
