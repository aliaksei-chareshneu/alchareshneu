## Safe instructor path (every progression explicit)
| Stage | Practical proof | What candidate may do | Clear boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Adult member opt-in | Adult identity/availability, informed choice, current club relationship | Receive role description | No appointed shifts, no expectation of free labour |
| 1. Safety foundation | STOP rule, matched safe intensity, protective gear checks, venue range and first-aid escalation explained; checks recorded | Observe and help prepare supervised equipment | No teaching, sparring marshaling or unsupervised child group |
| 2. Shadow a club session | **Two actually held** sessions with qualified mentor present, private mentor feedback | Help with setup, beginner greeting, spot simple unsafe situations | Not a substitute for confirmed instructor |
| 3. Assisted micro-teaching | **Two distinct safe 10–20 minute segments**: one general movement/control, one small-team communication under direct supervision; handle STOP drill | Explain a beginner exercise and facilitate opt-outs while mentor controls session | No independent role, no archery unless separately assessed |
| 4. Supervised half-session | Written rubric: safety **2/2 mandatory**, clarity ≥1, adaptive teaching ≥1, inclusion ≥1, group control ≥1, response/debrief ≥1 (other categories 0–2); minimum **8/12** including mandatory safety | Lead 30–45 min **while mentor remains responsible** | Assessment is local instructor competence, NOT B/C/K advancement |
| 5. Responsible instructor by date | Signed owner/board approval, competency for weapons/age, current venue, insurance, participant protections, session plan, backup and emergency plan | Independently lead an **explicit assigned date** | No automatic recurring duties, no retroactive promotion, no pay promise |

**Rubric is a PILOT proposal:** any change to club-approved qualifications/ranks and formal roles needs separate founder/board approval. A command rank C is not instructor certification. A temporary O duty is not a C rank.

## Safety-specific gates
- For foam-LARP drills, standards must be confirmed for venue/event-specific equipment and ages.
- No thrusts if rules prohibit them; STOP/controlled target zones always.
- Archery requires a **separately assessed** competent range leader, backstop, safe line, barrier, emergency stop and defined range. General foam sword competence never authorizes archery leadership.
- No minors assigned to staffing; interaction with minors requires guardian consent and age-appropriate safeguards.
- Risk of concussion/injury: stop, refer first aid and follow agreed incident procedure; never push injuries out of the way to hold class.
- Always reserve right to cancel a session if an eligible instructor, venue, insurance, equipment or sufficient supervision is unavailable.
- Do not advertise a confirmed Saturday teaching shift until it is independently verified and entered in `Druzina_Training_Capacity_Gate`.

## Fairness and monetization
- Shadowing is voluntary and individually agreed; no coerced unpaid ongoing work.
- Prior to a remunerated shift, agree fee/rate, who is legal service provider (z. s. or OSVČ), date, cancellation, employer/contractor/tax issues, insurance and responsibility **in writing**.
- Do not introduce a paid package or make an internal instructor training “rank-up” purchasable without current legal product approval.
- Trainer quality and member retention, not paid conversions alone, decide continuation.
