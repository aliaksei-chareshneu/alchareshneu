## Authorisation gate
Publicly list adults 18+ only when `Age_Class=ADULT`, `Name_Opt_In=TRUE`, `Stats_Opt_In=TRUE`, `Awards_Opt_In=TRUE`, `Visibility_Status=PUBLIC_APPROVED`, proof/approval dates and approving person exist, and no revocation/expiration is active. For portraits also require `Photo_Opt_In=TRUE`, a separately approved photo path, and an approved image uploaded only into `druzina/leaderboard/images/portraits/` (safe basename .webp/.png/.jpg). Public source data never contains Member_ID, email, phone, guardian details, evidence, payment, health, WhatsApp messages or legal consent references. Do **not** automatically list minors even if guardian agrees: a separate child-safe private progress view should be developed later.

## Activity evidence
Only rows with `Verification=VERIFIED`, `Approved_For_Count=TRUE`, `Verified_By`, `Verified_At`, `Proof_Ref`, `Unique_Key` and valid completed event date count. Duplicate member/event/type suppressed. Accepted canonical `Event_Type` = `TRAINING`, `ARCHERY`, `BATTLE`, `VOLUNTEER`, `MENTOR`. Approved ARCHERY also counts as one general training. Seven categories: training attendance, battle visits, archery sessions, instructor-verified skill milestones, approved volunteer minutes, mentor sessions and distinct ISO weeks of verified attendance. Each shows **this month** and **all-time** totals; all-time is not fabricated from past schedules. This is participation only, never "best fighter skill", official rank, purchase, training level, or mandatory volunteering.
