Družina Moravy — HEMA Fencing & LARP Club in Brno

A club for historical medieval combat, fencing (HEMA), archery, and LARP re-enactment in Brno.

Družina Moravy revives the living tradition of Great Moravia — fellowship, craft, and the path from novice to warrior — so that everyone who joins finds roots, mastery, and their people.

PRO FIRMY A SOUKROMÉ SKUPINY · BRNO

Obránci hradu — teambuilding, který vás spojí

90 minut příběhového týmového zážitku s instruktorem vedeným historickým šermem pro 8–20 dospělých. Jednoduché role, signály, měkčené tréninkové meče a společná „obrana brány“; bez tvrdého sparringu a bez nutnosti předchozích zkušeností.

Orientačně 7 900 Kč za celou skupinu do 20 lidí (při plné skupině 395 Kč na osobu).

FOR COMPANIES & PRIVATE GROUPS · BRNO

Castle Defenders — teamwork with a story

A supervised 90-minute, beginner-friendly historical-fencing experience for 8–20 adults. Roles, signals, padded practice swords and a cooperative castle-gate challenge. No hard-contact sparring.

Indicative CZK 7,900 for the whole group of up to 20 (CZK 395 each at full capacity).

Payment

How Payment Works#

  • Registration and payment must be completed at least 48 hours before the start of training, via Revolut: revolut.me/aliaksj5pq.
  • Payment later than 48 hours before, or on the day — a 25% surcharge on the session price.
  • Need to borrow/rent club gear (weapons, armour)? Tell us at least 48 hours before training, same as payment. Later — the same 25% surcharge on the session price.
  • Payment (including any late surcharge) must be received before the training starts — without it, you won't be admitted to that session.
  • Training takes place only if at least one participant has a paid session (including a first-time trial) or an active subscription. If no one qualifies — the session is cancelled.

Rank

The Member's Path#

Rank tracks accumulated progress based on attendance and bringing people in. Each rank grants a specific colored bracelet, awarded in person and announced out loud at the end of training.

RankBraceletThresholdPerk
I — Recruit (Otrok)No braceletShowed up to a trainingBasic access. At your very first training you can try any weapon in the club.
II — Warrior (Ratník)Red8 sessionsPriority booking on club trips + 5% discount on custom craft/repair.
III — Veteran (Veterán)Blue20 sessions + 2 battlesA pin badge · priority choice of sparring partner in free rounds · access to the special trip "Battle Under the Mountain" + 10% discount.
IV — Champion (Vítěz)Orange40 sessions + 5 battlesPriority access to the club's best/newest gear + 15% discount.
V — Warlord (Tisícník)Black with a gold thread100 sessions, 1+ year tenure, 10 battlesRuns trainings independently when the head trainer is away + 20% discount.

The sparring-partner priority (Rank III) only applies in free rounds.

Rules and code

Conduct & Safety#

Conduct

  • No extremism — immediate removal.
  • Respect for members, gear, and the venue.
  • Honor code — honestly acknowledge hits taken in sparring.
  • Conflicts are resolved through the head trainer, not in public.
  • Alcohol and drugs are strictly forbidden.

Safety

  • The right to refuse a bout — no explanation needed.
  • Gear check is mandatory before use.
  • Newcomers get a briefing before their first bout.
  • Children from age 7, with written parental consent.
