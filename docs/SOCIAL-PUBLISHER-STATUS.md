# Social Publisher (staged, not deployed)

Goal: turn one reviewed Community Hub event into posts across Telegram channels and officially supported Meta Page/Instagram surfaces, with per-destination receipts. Personal Facebook and WhatsApp group/channel updates require owner action; YouTube video uploading needs video/media rights and owner OAuth; TikTok direct publishing needs app approval. No use of account passwords, user-bots, or bulk unsolicited messaging.

Source: existing Community Hub `Events` sheet. All external posting disabled until credentials, permissions and explicit event approval are checked. Legacy Metricool scheduled items need duplicate checks before any new post. Do not commit private user data or tokens.
